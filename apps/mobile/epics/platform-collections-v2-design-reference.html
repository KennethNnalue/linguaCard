Refined for the codebase we have now — and a UX that motivates, not just lists

Since v1: the Global Word Dictionary shipped, the admin import shipped, and platform-collection words became dictionary references. That makes adoption instant and AI-free (words + audio pre-exist) and unlocks the headline UX upgrade: every Explore card shows exactly how many of its words you already know — an honest endowed-progress signal computed from a real join. The browse surface adopts the shelf grammar Story Studio already taught users, defaults to your level, and pairs every set with platform stories you can read right now.

✕ Collections invisible to users (no public API/UI) ✕ Imports stuck unpublished (no toggle) ✕ v1 cards showed cost ("28 words"), not proximity
✓ Explore: topic shelves + level chips ("for you" default) ✓ "12 of 28 known" progress on every card ✓ Get words — instant, audio included ✓ Related stories on every set ✓ Admin publish lifecycle

Fresh → Partial → Adopted. Ring fill = knownCount/wordCount (exact join — never estimated, never inflated; ADR-3). The Get button names the post-dedup delta. Adopted swaps to the user collection's live mastery bar and deep-links into My Sets.

Level chips + "for you" default

Same chip primitive as the Vault toolbar and Story Explore. suggestedLevel (mode of the user's dictionary-linked card levels) renders first, marked and pre-selected; "All" is one tap away. Override persists across launches (LC-412).

Topic shelf

Header emoji · Topic · See all › over a horizontal scroll row. Card width ~132px in the 280px mock (≈160px at 375px viewport) so ≥2.2 cards peek — the affordance that invites scrolling, identical to the shipped Story Explore shelves (ADR-4). See-all opens a vertical grid scoped to topic + active level.

Detail word grouping

New here (16) leads — it's what adoption buys. Already yours (12) follows at reduced opacity with a ✓ YOURS chip, collapsed beyond three rows. Every row has 🔊 that plays immediately: the word is dictionary-linked, so audio already exists.

Adopt = instant (the architecture is the UX)

Platform words are dictionaryWordId refs → adoption copies dictionary content into cards and stamps the link. Zero AI calls, zero TTS, no progress screen needed — just the toast with the honest post-dedup count: "16 words added — audio included ✓". Generate-story pre-seeds the existing sheet; the generation pipeline is untouched.

Related stories shelf

Matched deterministically: collection's admin-set storyCategory + level → GET /platform-stories (existing endpoint, limit 6). Gives the learner context before a single card is reviewed — read the words now, generate a personal story after adopting. Hidden when no category is set (ADR-5).
