3. Web push reminder (LC-359, LC-365)
Sent by the backend hourly cron only when the daily goal is unmet, at the user's chosen local time, at most once per day. Fires even when the tab is closed (Web Push, not the in-page Notifications API — ADR-4). The copy references the live streak count and remaining cards.
