User Onboarding & First-Run Activation — Design Reference (LC-ONB-01 → LC-ONB-18)
Flow at a glance
Five full-screen, skippable steps run right after registration, then drop the user onto Home with real cards and an activation checklist. Welcome → Motivation → Level → Seed vault → Goal & finish. Every step has Skip; progress is persisted to user_settings.onboardingStep so a dropped session resumes where it left off.
Design tokens are identical to the rest of the app. All colors, type, spacing, and radii reference the LDS :root variables (brand #2D5A4E, accent #E07B3F, Lora display / DM Sans body). Buttons map to <lc-button>, empty states to <lc-empty-state>, persona chips to <lc-category-chip>. No raw hex/px in the eventual component SCSS — this mockup just previews the result.
1. Welcome (LC-ONB-07)

RTL & accessibility (LC-ONB-17)
RTL: Arabic (ar) flips automatically — LanguageService sets <html dir="rtl">. The Back chevron, progress dots, persona grid, and level rows all mirror. Reduced motion: step transitions and the adopting spinner respect prefers-reduced-motion. A11y: each step focuses its heading on entry; persona chips and level cards expose aria-pressed; Skip/Back carry translated aria-labels. All copy resolves through the onboarding.* i18n namespace (parity across all 6 bundles).
