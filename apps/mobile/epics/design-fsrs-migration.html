3. Mastery band definitions (LC-149 / ADR-4)
Each level maps to a stability threshold. "Mastered" requires 180+ days of demonstrated stability — not just 8 button taps.

Stability-based mastery scale

Stability = days until FSRS predicts recall drops below 90%

4. Key behaviour changes
Side-by-side of what changes in the product as a result of the migration.
Behaviour SM-2 (current) FSRS (after migration)
Max interval after 10 "Easy" reviews 20,172 days (55 years) 365 days (hard cap)
Rating buttons 6 buttons: Blank / Hard / Hmm / Good / Easy / Nailed 4 buttons: Again / Hard / Good / Easy
Interval preview Not shown Shown per button after card flip
"Mastered" meaning Reviewed ≥ 8 times (any rating) Stability ≥ 180 days (proven retention)
Word detail stats Reviews / Avg rating / Interval / Last seen Reviews / Recall chance / Stability / Last seen
Ease hell risk High — easeFactor can lock at 1.3 permanently None — difficulty is bounded and self-correcting
Algorithm adapts to learner No — same formula for everyone Yes — per-card difficulty (D) updates each review
Review efficiency Baseline (SM-2) ~25% fewer reviews for same retention
